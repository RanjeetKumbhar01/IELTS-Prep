📋 IELTS Academic Quick Reference
🎧 Listening — 30 min · 40 Q
Part 1: Social conversation · Part 2: Social monologue · Part 3: Academic discussion · Part 4: Academic lecture
📖 Reading — 60 min · 40 Q · 3 Passages
14 question types: Multiple Choice, T/F/NG, Y/N/NG, Matching Headings, Matching Info, Matching Features, Sentence Endings, Sentence Completion, Summary Completion, Note Completion, Table Completion, Flow-chart, Diagram Label, Short Answer
✍️ Writing — 60 min · 2 Tasks
Task 1: Describe visual (≥150 words) · Task 2: Essay (≥250 words, more weightage)
🗣️ Speaking — 11–14 min · 3 Parts
Part 1: Intro topics (4–5 min) · Part 2: Cue card (1 min prep, 1–2 min speak) · Part 3: Abstract discussion (4–5 min)
