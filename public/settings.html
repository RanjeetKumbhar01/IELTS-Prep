IELTS Academic Format Quick Reference
Listening — 30 min · 40 Q
Part 1: Social conversation · Part 2: Social monologue · Part 3: Academic discussion · Part 4: Academic lecture
Reading — 60 min · 40 Q · 3 Passages
Academic passages with questions like Multiple Choice, True/False/Not Given, Headings, Sentence/Note Completion.
Writing — 60 min · 2 Tasks
Task 1: Visual data summary (≥150 words) · Task 2: Formal Essay response (≥250 words)
Speaking — 11–14 min · 3 Parts
Part 1: Introduction & general topics · Part 2: Individual cue card presentation · Part 3: Analytical discussion
