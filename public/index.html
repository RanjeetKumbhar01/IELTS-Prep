Guide to Use This Website

This platform is designed to simulate the actual online computer-delivered IELTS exam. Follow this recommended workflow:

Step 1

1. Book PDF on Left

Open your practice book PDF (e.g. Book 14 Test 1) on the left side of your screen.

Step 2

2. This Website on Right

Open this platform on the right side to record answers. This avoids handwriting to train for the online IELTS exam.

Step 3

3. Ready to Practice

We cannot host copyrighted books. Simply enter the book & test numbers, and start practicing!
